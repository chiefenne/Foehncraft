- **Species transport of an ideal-gas mixture (steam furnace stage SF1).** `[species]` names the species of a mixture from the 53 of GRI-Mech 3.0; N − 1 mass fractions are transported as fields `Y_<name>` with the flow's density, the closure species is 1 − Σ of them, so Σ Y = 1 in every cell. Diffusion by Schmidt or Lewis numbers and a turbulent Schmidt number; bounded convection by the MLP limiter, no clipping. Compositions in mole or mass fractions per inlet (`[boundary.<name>.species]`). With `[energy] density = "variable"` the density follows the mixture's molar mass, and with `properties = "mixture"` the energy equation and the flow take the mixture's c_p (NASA-7), λ (Eucken, Mathur) and μ (Chapman–Enskog, Wilke). The ledger gets an account per species and per element (C, H, O, N, Ar). New single-source kernel module `foehncraft_kernels::species`; codes SPE001 to SPE003. Examples `species_binary_diffusion` (exact decay, L2 error 9.5·10⁻⁶) and `species_coaxial_jet` (a methane jet in air, element accounts closed to 6.5·10⁻⁹), gallery and recipe 278.
