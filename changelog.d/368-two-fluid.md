- **Two-fluid (Euler–Euler) model for bubbly flow (L3 stage MP3, D352 to D356).** `[two_fluid]` adds a dispersed gas of one bubble size to the liquid of `[fluid]`: one momentum equation per phase, one pressure from the volume continuity of both, in the shared CPU/GPU kernels. Forces: Ishii–Zuber drag, implicit between the phases by partial elimination; Tomiyama lift with near-wall damping (Shaver & Podowski); Hosokawa wall lubrication; Burns turbulent dispersion, implicit in the gas fraction, with a grid-scale limit in nearly empty cells; Sato bubble-induced viscosity. Fields `volume_fraction` (α_g) and `gas_velocity_x/y/z`; line files gain `alpha_gas`, `u_gas`, `v_gas`, `w_gas`. A uniform bubble column gives the drag-balance slip to four digits and converges in two outer iterations per step; bubbly up-flow in a 57 mm pipe puts the wall peak where Wang et al. (1987) measured it and the core within 3 % at j_G = 0.4 m/s (39 % low at 0.1), with peaks about twice as high as measured. The mass check of a two-fluid run is the boundary volume account of both phases. New cases `two_fluid_column`, `bubbly_pipe_wang_jg01`, `bubbly_pipe_wang_jg04`, study `examples/studies/bubbly_pipe`, tool `tools/plot/two_fluid.py`. Gallery and recipe 368.
