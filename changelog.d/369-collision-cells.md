- **Collision cells of their own (D379).** `[spray.collision] cell = <edge in m>` lets the drops meet in cubes turned and shifted at random every step (Hou and Schmidt 2006) instead of the gas cells. ECN Spray G G1 uses cubes of 0.6 mm: drops 15 mm from the tip 10.8 µm (was 13.0; measured 7 to 10), liquid at 0.3 ms -1 % (was +20 %), at 0.8 ms +10 % (was +4 %). Smaller cubes merge more; the size dependence is an open gap. The Post outcomes are now D378 (were D349, a number already taken). Gallery and recipe 369.
