- **Radiation walk 16 % faster per bundle, results bitwise unchanged (RA0 part 1).** The tetrahedron walk of `foehncraft_kernels::walk` evaluates the distance of the current point only for the faces the target lies behind (new `walk::side`, the same operations as `sides`) and wraps the edge index without integer division. The furnace example on a 112k-cell mesh gives byte-identical `cells.csv` and `walls.csv` and takes 21.2 µs per bundle on 4 threads instead of 25.3. The cost per bundle grows about with the cube root of the cell count (80 µs at 858k cells): the ray crosses more cells.
