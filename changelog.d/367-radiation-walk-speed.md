- **Radiation walk about 2.3 times faster per bundle on tetrahedral furnace meshes (RA0).** Two steps. (1) The tetrahedron walk of `foehncraft_kernels::walk` evaluates the distance of the current point only for the faces the target lies behind (new `walk::side`) and wraps edge indices without integer division: bitwise the same results, 16 % faster. (2) A cell that is convex with planar faces is now crossed by the planes of its faces, with no tetrahedra: `Locator` stores the plane of every face and a convex flag per cell (checked to 1e-9 of the cell size, the apex inside every plane also checks the orientation), the walk uses them through `WalkMesh::plane` and `convex`, and cells that are warped or not convex keep the tetrahedron walk (the two meet at the faces, so every mixed mesh works). The tetrahedron state of a walk is found only when a tetrahedron step or the end of a walk needs it. On the box furnace the time per bundle on 4 threads falls from 25.3 to 10.7 µs (112k tetrahedra) and from 80 to 37 µs (858k tetrahedra); the tallies differ from the tetrahedron walk by round-off (29 of 20 412 wall faces, at most 3e-6 W), the energy account still closes. The plane arrays go to the GPU with the other walk arrays (`TransportArgs::plane`, `convex`), so CPU and GPU use the same walk. `Locator::set_plane_walk(false)` or the environment variable `FOEHN_NO_PLANE_WALK` returns to the tetrahedron walk for checks. New test `the_plane_walk_gives_the_chords_of_the_tetrahedron_walk` on tetrahedral, hexahedral, polyhedral and mixed meshes.
