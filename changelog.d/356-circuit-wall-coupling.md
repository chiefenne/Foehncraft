- **Water and steam circuits behind the walls (steam furnace SF7c, D349).** `[circuits]` couples a boiler circuit of SF7b to the wall boundaries of a steady flow with the energy equation. Each face takes the water side of its tube segment as an implicit Robin condition (U from the inner heat transfer coefficient, the tube metal and fouling). The circuit is solved again every few outer iterations from the wall heats until both agree, and the ledger pairs the wall heat with the circuit's own account. Outputs `circuit.csv` and `circuit/` (result, profiles, every mapped face). A slab on a water tube matches an independent march of the heat balance to 1.2·10⁻⁴ K. In a convective evaporator pass, two water walls share one drum loop (steam 0.142 kg/s, the hotter bank takes 125 kW and 11.2 kg/s). Codes CIR005, CIR006. Gallery and recipe 356.
