- **Near-body refinement with hanging-node faces (L13 stage 2f).** `[immersed.refine]` halves the hexahedra near the immersed bodies up to `levels` times before the cut (`layers` cells per level, or `distance` in metres), 2:1 balanced across faces, edges and corners, deterministic and serial; the faces between levels carry the hanging nodes, planar meshes are refined in their plane, other cell types are kept whole, and the IMM206 note gives the cells per level and the quality. Volume and boundary area are kept to round-off, also on perturbed meshes; a manufactured conduction solution stays second order across the hanging faces, and the Schäfer–Turek cylinder on a D/10 box mesh refined to D/40 near it reaches the lift of the uniform D/40 mesh with a seventh of its cells (gallery 361).
