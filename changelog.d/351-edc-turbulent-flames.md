- **Turbulent flames with the eddy dissipation concept and the eddy dissipation model (steam furnace stage SF3, D349).** `[chemistry] interaction = "edc"` gives every hot cell of a RANS flow its fine structures (γ* = (C_γ (ν ε/k²)^¼)², τ* = C_τ (ν/ε)^½, Magnussen 2005 constants 2.1377 and 0.4083; `edc.version = "1981"` takes γ_λ³) and burns them over τ* with the stiff integrator of SF2; the mean rate is ρ κ (Y* − Y), κ = γ*/(τ* (1 − γ*)), γ* held at `edc.max_fine_fraction` (0.75). `interaction = "edm"` is the eddy dissipation model of Magnussen & Hjertager (1977) as the fallback (A = 4, B = 0.5, capped by the Arrhenius rate; mechanisms whose reactions run one way; a reaction and its exact reverse, such as the CO oxidation and CO₂ dissociation of Westbrook & Dryer, are capped by mixing as a pair). Steady and transient: transient steps take the exact increment (1 − e^(−κΔt))(Y* − Y), which conserves every element; steady runs renew the rates every `update_every` outer iterations, relax the rate at the current state with `relaxation`, put the sinks on the diagonal of the species rows and give the energy a heat release linear in the species, taken with the mass fractions of each energy refresh. The chemistry kernel and its GPU entry take a residence time per cell. New fields `heat_release_rate`, `fine_structure_fraction`, `fine_structure_time`; `chemistry.csv` gains the failed cells, the cells at the guard and the range of τ*; new warning CHM004 (fine-structure reactor failed in some cells); CHM003 now refuses a turbulence model without an interaction, an interaction without one, and steady laminar cases. New cases `jet_flame_edc_jl4.toml` and `jet_flame_edm_wd2.toml` (the piloted methane jet flame with the geometry and inlets of Sandia Flame D, Barlow & Frank 1998), plot `tools/plot/jet_flame_fields.py`; gallery and recipe 351.
