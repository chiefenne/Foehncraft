- **Chemistry in the cells of a flow (steam furnace stage SF2b, L2, D120).** `[chemistry]` lets the species of a transient laminar flow react: each time step integrates every cell as a constant-pressure reactor with the stiff RODAS3 kernel of SF2a (Lie splitting), and the changes of the mass fractions and the heat release become sources of the species and energy equations on the variable density. `[chemistry.flame_speed]` reports the burning velocity of a premixed flame from the fuel consumption and can check it against a band; `chemistry.csv` has the heat release and the net production of every species per step. A laminar stoichiometric methane–air flame with GRI-Mech 3.0 and species Lewis numbers burns at 0.348 m/s, against the measured 0.360 ± 0.017 m/s; the 4-step mechanism of Jones & Lindstedt at 0.430 m/s. The cell body is one function for the CPU threads and the GPU: `[run] backend = "cuda"` integrates the cells with the PTX entry `chemistry_react`, and the example `chemistry_gpu` of foehncraft-cuda compares the two bit for bit; `python3 -m tools.bench.chemistry_cloud` and `tools/notebooks/sf2_chemistry_colab.ipynb` run it on a cloud GPU. Also fixed in the species of SF1: nitric oxide was missing from the GRI-Mech species data (a YAML 1.1 reader had turned `NO` into false), species with brackets such as `CH2(S)` now give valid field names (`Y_CH2_S`), and the mixture viscosity computes its pair factors once (a time step of 53 species went from 10 s to 0.5 s). Steady and turbulent reacting flows follow with SF3. Gallery and recipe 298.
