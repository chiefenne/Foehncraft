- **Injector devices drawn with the spray (ID2, ID3, D343 to D345).** A `[[spray.device]]` may carry a surface: `geometry = "standard-gasoline"` or `"standard-diesel"` (two tips of our own, drawn from published sizes by `tools/data/injector_standard.py`, in `assets/injectors/` with a README; a standard tip without holes of its own brings its default holes, 8 at 37° and 7 at 77.5°) or `"stl"` with `file`, `scale` and, for a file drawn in another frame, `stl_origin`, `stl_axis`, `stl_reference`. Each run with a device writes `spray/devices.vtu` once (the placed surface, one line per hole along its axis, the cone of each injector) and lists it in `series.json` under the new key `static` (format version unchanged); `result.json` gets `spray.devices` with the frame and every hole. A user STL is checked (turned-over, degenerate, duplicate triangles: SPR010, not repaired). New tool `spray.place` writes the placed device without a run. The device is a picture, not a wall (D344). `spray_g_device_injectors.toml` gives the Spray G injector as a device on the standard gasoline tip; gallery and recipe PRNUM.
