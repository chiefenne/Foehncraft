- **Multi-rank MPI, step W7: species, the variable density and statistics on ranks.** `foehn flow case.toml --ranks N` (and `[run] rank_count`, `mpirun -n N foehn flow`) now also runs species transport (stage SF1, with the mixture properties), the variable density of stage 7c (an ideal gas or a liquid whose density follows the temperature and the mixture) and `[output.statistics]` (field moments, probe and coefficient statistics, phase averages), steady and transient, with every output file byte for byte that of one rank. The MLP vertex limiter (D237, the default of species) runs on ranks: each rank bounds the vertices of its own cells and the ranks that share a vertex combine their bounds (a minimum and a maximum, the same in any order); its ε scaling by cell volume sums the domain volume over all ranks. The species summary and element accounts, the density summary (mass, extremes, Mach number) and the history columns of the variable density are computed over all ranks. Fixed on the way: the field statistics (`[output.statistics] fields = true`) of a run with passive scalars, species or the energy equation stopped with an index error on one rank as well (the moments were named after the passive scalars only); they now cover every transported field (scalars, species, temperature). Fixed for every run on ranks: a parabolic or other profile inlet split between ranks took the extent of each rank's own part of the boundary, so its profile differed from one rank (gallery 279 and 290 had their inlets on one rank); the extent of the whole boundary is now taken from the global mesh. Still refused on ranks: a closed volume (p₀ from the mass), the thermal acceleration, cycle loop and frozen-flow march of conjugate runs, and what `[run] ranks` refuses (turning zones, solid and fluid regions, gravity, ζ-f and EB).
