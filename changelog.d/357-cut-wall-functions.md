- **Wall functions on cut bodies (L13 stage 2c).** A turbulent case with wall functions now runs on cut bodies: every cut and merged wall cell takes the exact distance of its centroid to the body, the wall function takes its velocity from the cell one layer out along the wall normal (`[immersed.cut] wall_sample`, default on), the cut wall faces carry the wall shear stress without a non-orthogonal correction, and LIM011 lists the y⁺ range of every body. New body shape `plane` (a half-space with `centre` and `normal`). The wall distance of the turbulence models takes the exact distance to cut bodies too. A plate tilted by 2° against the mesh gets C_f within 7 % of White's correlation from s = 0.5 on (gallery 357).
