- **Drops can bounce (D349).** `[spray.collision] outcomes = "post"` adds the outcomes of Post and Abraham (2002) to the drop collisions: bouncing below the Weber number of Estrade et al. (1999) and reflexive separation above that of Ashgriz and Poo (1990), in front of O'Rourke's coalescence and grazing. ECN Spray G G1 uses it: drops 15 mm from the tip 13.0 µm (was 14.5; measured 7 to 10), liquid lead +4 % at 0.8 ms (was +9 %). Gallery and recipe 363.
