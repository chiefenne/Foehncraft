- **Conditions on cut bodies (L13 stage 2e).** `patches` on an STL body turns the solids of an ASCII file, or the attribute tags of a binary file, into boundaries `<body>-<patch>` with any condition (inlets, outlets, walls). `omega` turns the wall of a cut body about its axis and reports the torque in the boundary summary. `invert` keeps the fluid inside a cylinder, sphere or box. Taylor–Couette flow with both cylinders cut out of a box mesh converges at second order in the velocity, with the torque within 0.23 % on 176 cells across; a cylinder blows through a slot in the Re 20 channel (gallery 360).
