- **Setting changes keep the inlet conditions.** A re-read of the case file, a `[[schedule]]` entry and `run.set` of the control port now all keep the energy, scalar and species conditions of the boundaries through one shared step that the compiler checks for completeness; before, species inlet compositions (all three) and the energy and scalar conditions (`run.set`) were dropped and the inlet failed its validation. A changed species composition in a re-read file is reported as not applied.
