- **Evaporation and boiling in the volume of fluid (L3 stage MP2, D351).** A `[vof]` case now takes `[energy]`: each phase gives `specific_heat` and `conductivity`, and the mixture enters the energy equation (ρ c_p per cell, the conductivity in series). `[vof.phase_change]` adds evaporation and condensation by the energy of the interface: the interface cells are held at T_sat, the heat they take gives the rate, and the continuity takes the volume the vapour needs (T_sat and h_lv given, or IAPWS-IF97 at `pressure` for water). Water at 1 atm against exact solutions: the Stefan problem within 1.1 %, the sucking problem within 1.8 %, Scriven's bubble on the new sphere-sector mesh within 3.2 %; film boiling on a hot wall against Berenson. `vof.csv` gains `evaporated`, `phase_rate` and `interface_cells`, the field output `phase_change_rate`. The volume account of the run checks the boundary volume flow against the volume made, and BC017 no longer warns when a phase change drives the flow. New cases `vof_stefan`, `vof_sucking`, `vof_scriven`, `vof_film_boiling`, tool `tools/plot/vof_phase_change.py`. Gallery and recipe 358.
