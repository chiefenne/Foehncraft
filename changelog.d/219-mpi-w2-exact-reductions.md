- **Multi-rank MPI, step W2: exact reductions.** Sums, dot products, residual norms and min/max no longer depend on the order of the terms, the thread count or the number of ranks (D31 (1), D93 (4)). `foehncraft_core::exact::ExactSum` is a long accumulator over the whole binary64 range (Kulisch 1971; Collange et al. 2015), rounded once to nearest even, with NaN and infinities counted apart and a batched path (one integer bin per exponent). `Communicator::sum_exact`, `sum_exact_many` and `all_sum_i64` reduce it across ranks as one all-reduce of 71 integers; `min` and `max` are order-independent (`min_total`, `max_total`). The CPU backend and the flow CPU device compute dot products, norms and min/max exactly, the AMG eigenvalue estimate too, and about 30 sums over cells, faces and patches of the flow crate (balances, torques, mass flows, volumes, sources) are exact. One-rank results change once in the last bits against v0.7; no reference band is affected. **GPU:** the GPU keeps its block sums until v1.1 (owner decision of 2026-11-23), so CPU and GPU runs now agree to rounding, not bitwise. Partitioned `foehn mesh` runs check sums over the ranks against one rank (`reductions` in the report); the example `mpi_reductions` checks the same across 1 to 4 MPI processes in CI.
