- **Structural solver, part 3 (L53, MS5): dynamics and thermal loads.** Generalised-α (Chung & Hulbert 1993) and mode superposition with exact modal steps, Rayleigh damping from factors or from two damping ratios, modal damping ratios, and the Craig–Bampton reduction of a substructure (library). Structural cases take `[transient]`, `[damping]`, `[histories]` (tables and sines that scale loads in time) and `[[temperatures]]` (a temperature change and a difference across the thickness of beams and plates, through the material's `expansion`); `transient.csv` and snapshots in `structure.vtk`. Checked against the damped oscillator (second order, the high-frequency damping ρ∞, exact energy at ρ∞ = 1) and the free thermal expansion and bending of beams and plates (gallery and recipe MMM).
